Since 1999

Baking with Warmth, Heritage, and Uncompromising Love.

Mama Tunde’s Bakery didn't start in a big commercial facility—it started in a bustling family kitchen in Lagos with an old oven, a wooden rolling pin, and a mother determined to create cakes that tasted like pure celebration.

When Mama Tunde baked her first golden sponge cake for her son Tunde’s 7th birthday, neighbors could smell the sweet vanilla aroma down the street. Soon, friends and community members were knocking on her door, pleading for birthday cakes, meat pies, and tea loaves.

Over two decades later, our ovens have grown, but our recipe has never changed: 100% pure creamery butter, farm-fresh eggs, real cocoa, and patience. We still crack every egg by hand and fold every batter with the same warmth that started it all.

Our Philosophy

The Four Kitchen Rules We Never Break

Why a cake from Mama Tunde tastes distinctly different from mass-produced commercial bakeries.

🧈

Pure Dairy Butter

No industrial margarine, shortening, or artificial oil substitutions. You taste pure butter richness in every bite.

🌅

Dawn Baking

Our ovens light up at 4:30 AM every morning so morning pickups and deliveries are warm, fragrant, and never days old.

🎨

Custom Craftsmanship

From delicate chocolate drip cascades to hand-piped celebratory messages, every cake is tailored with personal artistry.

💬

Human Connection

No robotic automated phone systems. You talk directly with real bakers on Telegram who care about your special day.
